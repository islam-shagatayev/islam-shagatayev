# Islam Shagatayev

Legal Tech Officer at Unicase Central Asia, Astana. I am a lawyer who ships software: I build the internal tools my firm runs on, and I write most of them with AI coding agents.

My work sits where legal process meets automation. A due diligence check that used to take an hour, a market scan that used to take half a morning, a timesheet nobody filled in: each of those is a system now.
